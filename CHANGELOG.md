# What's new in MASKED

Newest first. Every version is on the [Releases](../../releases) page; the Android app (APK) is attached to the releases that have one.

| 2.29.25 | **Lots on sale at once**: one approval moves every lot to an output of its own (a chain of small transactions, the next from the previous one's change) and signs every lot straight away, so all of them are on sale even if MASKED is closed right after. A single Instant listing that needs a move is on sale at once too. Bitcoin added to the RGB balance goes in as a few coins (several buys in a row each have one), and a coin just spent is never picked again. A buyer before the moves confirm gets the tokens once they do. Needs the engine rebuilt (`engine/build.sh`, `masked-b5.patch`: `setOwnUnconfirmed`); each move keeps the rest of the tokens on a small RGB output of its own (no free RGB output needed: no more "Insufficient allocations") and sends the rest of the bitcoin back to the RGB balance; a token output with enough sats pays its own move; the Instant records never fill the browser's storage (finished entries lose their signed data, and the oldest go when it's full); with an older engine, lots go on sale one per block as before. Settings → RGB → What's in this RGB wallet: where each token's balance is (free, in listings, waiting for a block, unusable), every RGB output with its sats, tokens and what it's doing (on sale, going live, cancelling, free, waiting, unusable, empty), and every bitcoin coin of the RGB balance; sites can show the same with `rgb.getOutputs()` (asks first; with recent RGB activity). **Free spare outputs**: keeps 6 empty RGB outputs for receiving tokens and turns the rest, with the RGB wallet's bitcoin, into 4 coins for buys in one transaction (`rgb.freeSpare()`; needs the engine rebuilt: `spareUtxos`, `freeSpareUtxosBegin`). Listings whose signed data was dropped to free browser storage stay on sale and their sales are still seen |
| 2.29.24 | One copy of RGB at a time: before tokens move, are received or bought, MASKED checks the cloud backup isn't newer (another copy of the wallet) and the outputs it counts aren't spent on chain; Settings → RGB → Check RGB against the chain repairs a copy that fell behind. Instant listings travel with the RGB backup, so any copy of the wallet can finish or cancel them (and cancel one from its output alone). An Instant listing can sell your whole balance even when it sits on several outputs (they're joined in the listing's move). Lots move one per block (the first at once; a lot already alone on an output needs no move); RGB pauses moving tokens while another copy of the wallet saved newer RGB data, with a button to load that backup; tokens only move when the wallet is up to date with the chain. Loading an earlier cloud backup asks first, says what it leaves out, and saves it as the newest backup (it used to be refused as older, and the screen then offered the unwanted state back); the last 5 states replaced by loads are kept, with Undo a load; every kept backup version is listed. The next lot of a split no longer stops as "behind the chain" while the previous lot's own move waits for its block. A blind RGB invoice is never made on an output the chain shows already spent (tokens paid to it could never move again) |
