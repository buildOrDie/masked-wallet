# What's new in MASKED

Newest first. Every version is on the [Releases](../../releases) page; the Android app (APK) is attached to the releases that have one.

| Version | What changed |
|---|---|
| 2.32.7 | **No more closing the wallet to get unstuck**: approving a buy, a listing or a mint while MASKED was syncing said "Wait for the current RGB operation." or "Still preparing.", and only worked after closing the wallet. Now it waits for the sync to finish (a few seconds) and carries on by itself, showing "Waiting for your RGB wallet to finish updating…". RGB operations take turns instead of refusing each other |
| 2.32.6 | **Faster RGB refreshes**: every refresh asked the transfer server about every invoice still waiting for its transfer data, and a wallet with many old invoices (launchpad claims, test sends) waited on dozens of answers each time. Invoices waiting for over a day are now asked about once an hour (and on a full refresh), not every time; nothing is given up. **Clearer sends**: sending more of a token than is free right now says how much can go and where the rest is (waiting for its block after a cancel, move or receive, or in your listings), instead of "Insufficient total assignments". **Listed tokens aren't offered for sending**: tokens in your Instant listings counted as "Available to send" and Send stayed on, then failed; they're now left out, and the token's page says they're in your listings and to cancel a listing to send them. **Wallet data kept safe**: MASKED asks Chrome for unlimited storage, so Chrome never clears its data (the RGB wallet, NFT pictures) when the disk runs low; before, that storage was best-effort. NFT pictures no longer make the engine panic when read during a refresh. Needs the engine rebuilt (`engine/build.sh`, `masked-b5.patch`: `setRefreshSkip`) |
| 2.32.5 | **NFTs bought on the market now arrive**: a listing carried the NFT's picture but not its `metadata.json`, so the buyer's MASKED refused the NFT, though the purchase confirmed and the NFT sat on the buyer's output. Listings now carry every file of the NFT. **NFTs already bought come in**: on the next refresh MASKED rebuilds each refused purchase from the market's record of the sold listing and the purchase transaction, checks every file against the NFT and the transfer against the chain, and takes the NFT in. Needs the engine rebuilt (`engine/build.sh`, `masked-b5.patch`: `reopenPresignedReceive`; files the wallet holds are used before asking the transfer server) |
| 2.32.4 | **Several NFTs at once**: buy several NFTs of a collection with one approval (each checked, picture and traits, and bought in its own transaction; one someone else bought first is skipped, nothing paid), and list several NFTs with one approval (each signed as its own Instant listing). For sites: `rgb.buyInstantMany({ items, nft: true })` takes different NFTs, and the new `rgb.listInstantMany({ items, market })` |
| 2.32.3 | Settings shows the real version at the bottom (it said 2.3.3 whatever the version) |
| 2.32.2 | **Batch mints**: on a launchpad collection minted by its claimers, mint up to 10 NFTs in one go: one approval and one network fee for all of them. MASKED mints each NFT onto its own output of yours in the one transaction, and the launchpad checks every one before it adds its signature. If the wallet is closed halfway through taking them in, the next refresh goes on from where it stopped. Sites see the limit as `rgb.mintBatchMax`. No engine change |
| 2.32.1 | **Launchpad NFTs minted by their claimer**: on a launchpad collection set up this way, MASKED mints your NFT itself, from the collection's picture and traits, onto an output of yours in the claim's own transaction, and signs its part. The launchpad checks the NFT matches the collection exactly and adds the last signature while your claim holds; one approval, and you pay and receive in the same transaction, as before. The launchpad's server holds and mints no NFT at all, so a collection of any size costs it nothing, and a claim not completed in time can never go through. The NFT shows after one confirmation (engine: `udaGenesisFor`, `checkUdaGenesis`, `adoptUdaGenesis`). |
| 2.32.0 | **NFTs on Signet (RGB21 / UDA)**: MASKED holds, shows and sends RGB NFTs on test networks. Each NFT shows with its picture, which travels with the NFT itself: the sender posts it, encrypted for the recipient, next to the transfer, and the recipient checks it against the contract before accepting. **Cancel an NFT listing**: an NFT's Instant listing can be cancelled (the NFT moves whole to a new output of its own; it said "Invalid assignment"). **Collections**: Home lists NFT collections (one row each, with a few pictures and how many you hold), and each opens its own page of NFTs; an NFT's page goes back to its collection. An NFT on its way shows as incoming. **Verified collections**: like verified tokens, NFT collections are whitelisted (MASKED's launchpad: the "Verified" box of a collection); MASKED groups a verified collection by its exact asset IDs, marks it Verified and lists it first, and shows any other NFTs, grouped by ticker, as Unverified, so a copy named like a real collection never joins it. NFT pictures load once per opening, with one redraw for several (a missing picture was asked for again on every redraw). The NFT pages and grid turn on by themselves with an engine that has them. **Traits**: an NFT can carry a `metadata.json` next to its picture (its traits, rarity, description), fixed in the NFT like the picture; MASKED shows them on the NFT's screen. **Pictures kept compactly**: NFT files are stored once each, as raw bytes, apart from the RGB wallet's saved state (which is rewritten after every action), and as base64 in RGB backups instead of a list of numbers (3 to 4 times smaller). **NFT market**: NFTs are listed and bought as Instant listings, whole, at a fixed price: the seller signs once, the buyer buys alone in one transaction, and the buyer's MASKED checks the NFT before paying; the approval screens name the NFT. `rgb.getAssets` lists the NFTs held. A new NFT is always minted on an output of its own, so it can be listed at once (the launchpad's Instant claims). **Co-signed launchpad claims**: a launchpad's Instant claim comes without the launchpad's signature; MASKED checks it, signs its part and hands it back, and the launchpad adds its signature and sends it only while the claim holds, so a claim not approved in time can never go through later. The NFT is handed to the wallet once the launchpad has sent it (engine: `cosignedListingInfo`, `checkCosignedPartial`, `psbtExtractHexCosigned`, `cosignPresigned`, `presignedStripSeller`, `coloredAddress`). Not on mainnet. Wallets made before keep working: the NFT schema is added on open. **Fixes**: when another copy of the wallet saved newer RGB data, the approval screen offers "Load the latest RGB from the other copy and try again" under the error; a send refused because the asset's output is still tied to something else now says so (it used to say "not enough test BTC"), and a send of an NFT the other wallet hasn't accepted can be cancelled from the NFT's page; "What's in this RGB wallet" shows NFTs on their outputs; a site can ask for a higher network fee for an RGB step, never one below the wallet's own rate (a site's 0.5 sat/vB left a trade unconfirmed). Needs the engine rebuilt (`engine/build.sh`, `masked-b5.patch`: `issueAssetUda`, `mediaFile`, `mediaDigests`, `addMediaFile`) |
| 2.31.3 | **Tokens freed from trades that never finished**: a sale prepared on a market that the buyer never completed, an invoice nobody paid, a purchase the seller never sent used to hold the RGB output it would use, and every token on it, "waiting for a block" forever (the output unspent on chain). What's in this RGB wallet, and the token screen (See what holds them), now list every output held by an unfinished transfer and why, with a Release button when its transaction is on no block server and it's over an hour old; nothing is sent. Releasing happens by itself a day later. Markets can free a left or expired trade at once with `rgb.releaseTrade({ offerId, offer })`, like UniSat's cancelSwap. Needs the engine rebuilt (`engine/build.sh`, `masked-b5.patch`: `outputLocks`, `releaseUnbroadcast`) |
| 2.31.2 | **Errors you can act on**: when a listing, sale or buy fails because the wallet's view was behind the chain (a move or purchase that confirmed since: "Your tokens are still settling…"), MASKED catches up and tries again by itself; nothing was sent the first time. When something still fails, the approval screen shows what to do as a button right under the error: Refresh and try again, Fix it from the chain and try again (the Check RGB against the chain repair, without going to Settings), or Try again; a screen that couldn't be prepared has Refresh and check again. **Listing what's really free**: listing and selling count the tokens on this wallet's settled outputs (what What's in this RGB wallet shows as free), not only the engine's balance, which can lag after a move confirms ("You can list up to 45" while 149 were free). **Errors always on screen**: what went wrong, what's happening and the buttons to fix it now sit just above Reject / Approve, never at the bottom of a long screen you'd have to scroll. **No more "waiting for a block" forever on Signet**: when the block server in use says a transfer isn't confirmed, or reports a chain tip behind the real one, MASKED asks mempool.space before the RGB engine believes it (Signet only; on Mainnet the server you chose is never second-guessed). Tokens whose transfer confirmed days ago were held as "waiting for a block" and couldn't be listed or sold. memproxy.inscribe.dev is now the last Signet server tried |
| 2.31.1 | **Faster Instant buys and listings**: an Instant buy no longer waits for a whole RGB refresh before its screen is ready (it checks the coins it pays with and the listing on the chain itself), checks the listing on the block server while the wallet syncs, fetches its coins while it makes the invoice, checks the seller's token transfer once instead of twice, and returns as soon as the purchase is sent (the wallet's next refresh learns the rest; a sweep still waits between buys so the next one can use the change). Before a listing, the check that the wallet is up to date with the chain asks about 8 token outputs at a time instead of one after another. The console shows where the time goes (`[MASKED] Instant buy`, `[MASKED] Instant list checks`). **Fewer transaction requests**: a transaction the RGB engine fetched is kept for a month instead of a day, and once whichever block server it came from (switching servers, or a fallback server, fetched every transaction again); the console counts how many lookups went to the network (`[MASKED] RGB transaction lookups`). An Instant buy reads its coins' transactions from what MASKED already fetched, and hands the purchase to the wallet without asking the block server again for a transaction it accepted a moment ago. No wallet sync before an Instant buy (it synced twice: once on its own, once more listing the coins): it pays from the coins it already knows, each checked unspent on the block server at the same time, and syncs only when they aren't enough or one was spent. **Combined buys with one bitcoin coin**: a combined purchase needs two coins of the buyer's; with one coin that covers it all, MASKED uses one of its spare empty RGB outputs as the second (its sats come back in the change), so several listings still go in one transaction instead of one each. When listings can't be combined, the console says why (`[MASKED] Combined buy not possible`) |
| 2.31.0 | **Trade with UniSat wallets**: MASKED now speaks UniSat Wallet's pure P2P swap flow (UniSat 1.8.4+), so on P2P markets a MASKED wallet can buy RGB tokens from a UniSat seller and sell to a UniSat buyer, as well as trade with another MASKED. Seven steps, each side online in turn: the buyer makes a blinded invoice and drafts the transaction, the seller adds the token transfer and signs, the buyer checks the transfer and signs, the seller commits to exactly the transaction the buyer signed, the buyer checks that commitment and accepts, and the swap goes out. A buyer's tokens are only accepted once the seller's commitment checks out; the steps that follow from one you approved run by themselves. For sites: `rgb.p2pListable`, `p2pBuyPrepare`, `p2pSaleSign`, `p2pBuySign`, `p2pSaleCommit`, `p2pBuyAccept`, `p2pSaleFinish`, `p2pCancel` (docs/PROVIDER.md). **Instant listings, stricter**: an output that holds any other right of the token (inflation, replace, burn…) besides its tokens can't be listed. Needs the engine rebuilt (`engine/build.sh`, `masked-b5.patch`: `colorP2pSwap`) |
| 2.30.3 | **Sharing your RGB tokens is fast**: the "Share your RGB tokens" window is ready at once (it uses what MASKED saw in the last 10 minutes instead of catching up with the chain first), and a new "Share without asking next time" box (ticked on MASKED's own sites) lets a site get your token list straight away from then on, from what MASKED last saw, up to 30 minutes old. The list is kept in the browser's memory only, is cleared when you lock MASKED or close the browser, and stops when you disconnect the site. Home shows your last saved RGB balances the moment it opens, instead of "opening…" while the RGB engine starts. **A faster RGB refresh**: the engine reuses a sync from the last 20 seconds inside one refresh (it used to ask the block server again for every incoming transfer that confirmed, for cancelled invoices and coin lists) and asks the block server 8 addresses at a time instead of 5; the console shows where each refresh spends its time (`[MASKED] RGB refresh`). Needs the engine rebuilt (`engine/build.sh`) |
| 2.30.2 | **Lots in one transaction**: listing in lots makes every lot with one transaction instead of one per lot (10 lots: about 685 vB instead of about 2,550, about 70% less network fee). Every lot is signed at once as before. Needs the engine rebuilt (`engine/build.sh`, `masked-b5.patch`: `setSwapLots`); with an older engine, lots move one after another as before |

Every seller's signed listing stays as it is; each is checked like a single purchase, and the combined token transfer too, before anything is paid. When the listings can't be combined (the RGB balance has only one bitcoin coin, one was bought by someone else first, an older engine), MASKED buys them one after another as before. Needs the engine rebuilt (`engine/build.sh`, `masked-b5.patch`: `presignedCombinedBuy`) and the market updated to see each listing of a combined buy as sold |
