Pay 21 sats, read the article. No account, no card, no tracking.

Micropayments failed on the web because every payment needed an account, a card and a fee bigger than the price. Ecash changes the maths: a token worth 21 sats moves instantly, costs nothing to send, and the mint that issued it can't tell who paid whom.

This page asks MASKED for a token. You approve it in the wallet, the page gets the token, and a real site's server would redeem it at the mint before showing the rest. Here the demo just shows you what arrived.

The interesting part is the lock. A token can be locked to a Bitcoin taproot address: only the owner of that address can claim it. Paste it in the wrong chat, leak it in a log, and it's worth nothing to anyone else.
